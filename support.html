Duo Stretch Support

Duo Stretch lets web pages in Safari use the full width of iPhone Duo's outer display, running under Safari's controls on the right.

Turn it on

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps → Safari → Extensions.
  3. Tap Duo Stretch and turn it on.
  4. Under permissions, set All Websites to Allow, so it works on every site.
  5. In Safari, tap the page menu button (left of the address bar), then tap Duo Stretch and choose Wide. It stays on for every site until you choose Off.

Common questions

Nothing changes on my iPhone.

Duo Stretch works only on iPhone Duo's outer display. On the inner display, and on other iPhones, Safari already uses the full width, so the extension does nothing there. Also check that the extension is turned on, that it's allowed on all websites, and that Wide is selected.

A site still looks narrow.

Duo Stretch leaves two kinds of page alone: sites you've switched to desktop mode with Request Desktop Website, and sites built only for desktop. A site that pins its own bar to the right edge of the screen can also make Safari draw its control strip as a solid color.

Can I tap things under Safari's controls?

Safari's buttons stay on top, so taps on them go to Safari. Duo Stretch moves headers and bars that stick to the top or bottom of the page clear of the camera and the buttons.

How do I turn it off?

Tap Safari's page menu button (left of the address bar), then tap Duo Stretch and choose Off. Pages go back to Safari's normal width right away.

How do the trial and unlock work?

The free trial lasts 3 days and is started from the app. When it ends, Duo Stretch stops widening pages until you unlock it with a one-time purchase. Nothing is charged automatically, and there's no subscription. To restore an unlock on a new iPhone, open the app and tap Restore Purchases.
